Bio

I am an orchestral media composer. I love to write music that paint a vivid world using only the orchestra.

I'm from Indonesia and currently based in Jakarta. I started playing music since I was a kid, playing in a traditional Javanese orchestra called Karawitan, as well as choir throughout my teenage years. I started composing in 2022, took a break, and started again in 2025.

Currently, I'm learning to write to picture, aka media composition. I want to write music for animations, movies, or games. If you have any projects that need music, feel free to reach out to me by email~
